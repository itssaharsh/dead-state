# Brief — Dead State

1. **One-liner.** Dead State finds quests a model wrote that cannot be finished.
2. **Audience.** Hackathon judges. Watching once, scoring six criteria 1–5, three or more
   projects inside a 90-minute window. Possibly muted — so captions carry it too.
3. **The aha.** The third check flips to ✗ while the first two sit at ✓, on the *same* content —
   and the trap path draws in underneath: six ordinary player actions that brick two quests.
4. **Hook.** Result-first. "A model wrote four quests for one city. Every check you'd normally
   run says they're fine."
5. **Scope cut.** No architecture diagram. No provider rotation. No corpus walkthrough. No
   Hyper3D asset (not landed yet). No talk of what it *can't* do beyond the one honesty beat.

## Why this story and not a feature tour

The product's whole claim is a disagreement between three checks. A feature tour would show
three checks and imply "67% working". The film has to make the first two checks *feel like the
strawman they are* before the third lands — so the two passes are shown with the same weight a
normal tool would give them, and the turn is the field card that says "Both of those pass."

## The one thing that must not slip

Never say "proves". The search is sound over the *extracted* model, not the prose. The honesty
beat exists to say so on camera, because a judge who catches that unprompted will discount
everything else.
